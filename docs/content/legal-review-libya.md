# Privacy notice and terms of use: review against Libyan law (CD-10 input)

**Status (2026-11-18): engineering review, not legal advice, not an approval.** It maps each
"Pending" section of the two legal pages to the Libyan provisions found, and proposes draft wording
for a Libyan lawyer to accept, change or reject. The sections stay marked "Pending" in
`content/contact/copy.ts` and `content/pages/copy.ts`, so neither page can be published until CD-10
is signed off. Nothing here states that the site complies with any law.

## Sources

All texts read on lawsociety.ly (Libyan legislation database) on 2026-11-18:

| Law                                                                                                                                                        | Relevance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Law No. 6 of 2022 on Electronic Transactions** (قانون رقم 6 لسنة 2022 م بشأن المعاملات الإلكترونية), Chapter 7 "Protection of private data", Arts. 73–79 | The only personal-data rules found. **Art. 76**: anyone who controls personal data through electronic transactions must, **before processing**, inform the person by a specific notice of: the identity of the person responsible for processing, the nature of the data, the purpose, the methods and **locations** of processing, and what ensures safe processing. **Art. 78**: transfers of personal data **outside Libya** must consider an adequate level of protection, in particular: nature of the data, its source, purposes and duration of processing, the destination country, its international obligations and applicable law, and the security measures there. **Art. 79**: data may not be used for purposes other than those agreed without the person's consent; electronic documents may not be sent to a person who has expressly refused them; processing must not harm the person's rights or freedoms. Arts. 73, 75 and 77 (explicit consent, confidentiality, access and correction) are addressed to public bodies and certification-service providers. |
| **Law No. 5 of 2022 on Combating Cybercrimes** (قانون رقم 5 لسنة 2022 م بشأن مكافحة الجرائم الإلكترونية)                                                   | Unauthorised access and interference offences; **Art. 9**: producing, possessing, providing or using "encryption means" needs a licence or permit from the National Authority for Information Security and Safety. Counsel should confirm whether this reaches ordinary TLS/HTTPS and password hashing on a company website (Altamiuz is itself a security firm, so the question is worth asking).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Law No. 23 of 2010 on Commercial Activity** (قانون رقم 23 لسنة 2010 م بشأن النشاط التجاري)                                                               | Company registration and the Consumer Protection part (chapter "Informing the consumer"). Relevant to identifying the company and to terms of use; Altamiuz sells to organisations, so how far consumer rules apply is for counsel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

A search of the database for "البيانات الشخصية" (personal data) returned no stand-alone data
protection law. That shows only what the search found; counsel should confirm whether any other
instrument applies (and whether EU GDPR applies to visitors in the EU, FR-087).

Already drafted and consistent with Art. 76 (no change proposed): Scope, What we collect, Abuse
limits, Purpose, Access, Retention. The form's acknowledgement tick box records that the notice was
shown **before** submission, which matches Art. 76's "before processing".

Not done by the site, and correctly so under Art. 79: no newsletter or marketing e-mail is sent to
people who use the form.
