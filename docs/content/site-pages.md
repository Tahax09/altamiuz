# Site pages register (W-1)

Every public route is generated by the typed route map (`src/modules/i18n/routes.ts`); no route
was added. Pages are `pages` documents at `/{locale}/{slug}`; services and courses have their
automatic listings. `pnpm seed:pages` (after `pnpm seed:contact`) loads the pages below as
**drafts**; nothing is published by code. Copy: `content/pages/copy.ts` (reuses the homepage draft;
approval CD-11, Arabic review included).

## Site settings and menus (drafts filled by the seed only where empty)

- Header menu: About (Expertise, Standards and frameworks), Services, Training, Insights. Footer:
  Company and What we do columns. Services and Training use the new `route` link type.
- `contactPage`: header action, service/course calls to action and empty listings link to it while
  it is published in the language.
- `legalLinks`: privacy notice, terms of use. `listings.servicesIntro`: homepage services
  introduction; training introduction left empty until CD-05.
