### معيار نظام إدارة أمن المعلومات – أيزو 27001 / Information security management systems: ISO/IEC 27001

> ISO 27001 is an international standard published by the International Organization for
> Standardization (ISO). It describes how to manage information security in an organisation. The
> latest revision was published in 2013 as ISO/IEC 27001:2013. The first edition was published in
> 2005, developed from the British standard BS 7799-2.
>
> The standard's strength lies in its adaptability: it can be applied to every kind of
> organisation, profit or non-profit, private or state-owned, small or large. It was written by
> leading international information security experts and provides a methodology for implementing
> an information security management system in an organisation. Organisations can also obtain
> certification of their implementation (compliance) through an external audit of how they meet
> the standard's requirements. This means an independent certification body confirms that the
> organisation has implemented information security management in line with the standard.

**Outdated (CD-06):** the current edition is ISO/IEC 27001:2022, published in October 2022, which
replaced the 2013 edition. Certification to the 2013 edition ended with the transition period on
31 October 2025. Suggested replacement for the edition sentence: "The current edition is
ISO/IEC 27001:2022." (owner to confirm under CD-06).

### معيار أمن بيانات صناعة بطاقة الدفع الائتمانية / Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)

> The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) is designed to support and strengthen
> the protection of cardholder data, and to ensure international compliance against a single
> assessment measure.
>
> Compliance gives financial institutions, service providers and every party that handles
> cardholder data the core operational and technical requirements designed to protect cardholders'
> account data. That chain runs from the point of sale and the merchant to financial clients,
> issuers, service providers and the acquiring bank.

**Edition (CD-06):** state the edition the work follows (the current version is PCI DSS v4.0.1).
