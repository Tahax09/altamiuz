# V-2 visual direction: Contemporary Editorial Cybersecurity

**Status (2026-11-12): proposal for owner review. Not approved.** V-1 (an editorial CSS layer over
W-1) was rejected by the owner as generic, not rich enough, too conservative and weakly composed.
V-2 replaces the approach: the stylesheets `src/app/(site)/site.css` and `chrome.css` were
rewritten, V-1's `editorial.css` was removed, and the section renderer
(`src/ui/sections/Sections.tsx`) composes each block type differently. V-2 counts as approved
only after the owner has reviewed the screenshots.

## System

- **Surfaces**:
  - White page.
  - Inset rounded panels (`--radius-panel`, set `--inset` from the viewport edge):
    - **night** `#0b1533`: hero, methodology, contact introduction, detail-page call to action, footer;
    - **mist** `#eef0fb`: page headers, insights, statistics, legal outline, empty states;
    - **cobalt** `#3148c8`: the page-level call to action.
  - One accent only. Every text pair is AA or better; the ratios are in the token comments in
    `site.css`.
- **Type**: the constitution's typefaces: Manrope (display), Inter (text), IBM Plex Sans Arabic. A
  display step (`--step-5`, up to 5.75rem) with tight tracking in English, and a looser Arabic
  setting (no tracking, line height 1.3–1.4, wider measure). No new font files or weights, so
  the 120 KB per-language font budget is unchanged.
- **Labels**: eyebrows are pills with a dot (uppercase in English, sentence case in Arabic).
  Resource kinds are badges (shadcn/ui Badge pattern).
- **Numbered rail**: from 1024 px wide, every section with a header carries its position (01, 02 …)
  in a sticky circle in a narrow rail. It is decorative (`aria-hidden`); the heading carries the
  meaning.

## Motion (Motion for React, one client island: `src/ui/site/Enhancements.tsx`)

- Content below the fold enters once, on view: a 16 px settle with a 60 ms stagger. Image frames
  unmask downward (`clip-path`) without changing size. The methodology line is drawn from the
  start of the reading direction (right in Arabic) and the steps follow along the inline axis.
- Never animated: anything on screen at load (so no LCP delay), anything under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and anything without JavaScript. Nothing is hidden on the
  server; styles go through CSSOM/WAAPI, which the nonce-only CSP allows.
- Micro-interactions are CSS only, at 160 ms: arrow chips, card borders, pill fills, the FAQ
  plus/minus, and the menu caret.

## Script budget

The V-1 commit was 2.6 KB over the 150 KB page budget. That overage dated from W-1. V-2 cuts
script without raising the budget:

- The error boundary now ships its five strings instead of both full dictionaries
  (`src/modules/i18n/messages/status.ts`).
- The enquiry form's client code is loaded only where the form renders
  (`src/ui/enquiry/LazyEnquiryForm.tsx`).

Measured results are recorded in `specs/001-v1-website-cms/tasks.md` (V-2).
